About this book

In the Midlands town of Middlemarch around 1830, the idealistic Dorothea Brooke marries the elderly scholar Edward Casaubon, hoping to share in great work, and the ambitious young doctor Tertius Lydgate arrives with plans to reform medicine.

Their lives cross with a whole town’s worth of neighbours, debts and hopes, set against the Reform Act and the coming of the railway.
